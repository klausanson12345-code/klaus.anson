Optimizing Real-Time Object Detection for Traffic Cameras Using Knowledge Distillation (Smart Traffic System - ETLE)

International Conference on Information Technology, Information Systems and Electrical Engineering. Paper ID 1571316954.

Can a detector small enough for a Jetson Nano keep most of the accuracy of a much larger one? A YOLOv8-L teacher is distilled into a compact student, then tested on a subset of the BDD100K driving dataset.
